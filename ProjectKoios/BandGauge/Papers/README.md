# BandGauge publication crosswalks

This folder owns thin manuscript-specific metadata and, later, reviewed theorem instantiations. Reusable mathematics belongs in foundational BandGauge modules rather than being copied into paper modules.

The current `Ragasa2026A.lean`, `Ragasa2026B.lean`, and `Ragasa2026C.lean` files are metadata-only placeholders. Their compilation is not evidence that a paper-specific statement or complete publication claim has been encoded or proved. Generic foundational clauses linked from the crosswalk remain owned and counted in their foundational modules.
