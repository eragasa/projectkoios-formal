\section{Statement}
For every positive natural modulus $n$, map the two exact integer matrices entrywise into
$\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ and let them act on three-component column coordinates. The resulting
Bain-to-FCC and FCC-to-Bain maps are left and right inverses.
\section{Proof}
Entrywise reduction is a ring homomorphism, so it preserves both matrix products.
By \texttt{PKF-PHYSKIT-THM-001/002}, the two integer products are identity matrices.
Associativity of matrix-vector multiplication therefore makes both compositions the
identity on $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^3$.
