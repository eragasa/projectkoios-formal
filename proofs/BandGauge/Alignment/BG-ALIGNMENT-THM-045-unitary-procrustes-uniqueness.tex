\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $A,B\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$ and let exact data $Q,H$ satisfy
\[
  Q^*Q=I,\qquad H\succ0,\qquad A^*B=QH.
\]
For any unitary $R$, if
\[
  \lVert AR-B\rVert_F^2=\lVert AQ-B\rVert_F^2,
\]
then $R=Q$. Thus the supplied polar-witness minimizer is unique under the explicit
positive-definite-factor hypothesis.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
The objective has the fixed orientation $\lVert AR-B\rVert_F^2$, the action is on the right, and
the cross matrix is $A^*B$. The positive-definite premise is stronger than positive
semidefiniteness and excludes the null directions responsible for nonunique rank-deficient unitary
completions. No claim is made that a runtime tolerance correctly decides this premise.

\section{Proof structure}
Equality of objectives and the exact Frobenius/trace expansion imply equality of the real trace
scores. Set $W=R^*Q$, which is unitary. The polar factorization reduces score equality to
\[
  \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(WH)=\operatorname{tr}(H).
\]
Diagonalize $H=U\operatorname{diag}(d_i)U^*$ with every $d_i>0$. Conjugating $W$ by $U$ preserves
unitarity. The trace equality becomes
\[
  \sum_i d_i\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde W_{ii})=\sum_i d_i.
\]
Every diagonal real part is at most one. Strict positivity of every $d_i$ forces all diagonal real
parts to equal one. Unitarity then forces every diagonal entry to be one and every off-diagonal
entry to vanish, so $\widetilde W=I$, hence $W=I$ and $R=Q$.

\section{Rank-deficient and runtime boundaries}
Positive-semidefinite rank-deficient factors are intentionally outside this uniqueness theorem.
Their polar witnesses still prove global minimality through \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-THM-044}, but
Formal does not select an arbitrary unitary completion on a null space. The theorem authenticates
no SVD, rank threshold, runtime branch, binary64 value, optimizer, serialization, or certificate.
It establishes no physical applicability, scientific validation, novelty, or publication status.
