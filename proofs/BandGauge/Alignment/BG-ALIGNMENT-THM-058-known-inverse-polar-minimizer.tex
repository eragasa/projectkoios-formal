\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be finite and let $A_k,B_k\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$. Suppose exact polar-witness data
$Q_k,H_k$ satisfy
\[
  Q_k^*Q_k=I,\qquad H_k\succ0,\qquad A_k^*B_k=Q_kH_k
\]
for every $k\in K$. Let $O=(O_k)_{k\in K}$ be an exact unitary known-inverse control satisfying
\[
  A_kO_k=B_k\qquad\text{for every }k\in K.
\]
Then
\[
  O_k=Q_k\qquad\text{for every }k\in K,
\]
or equivalently the two unitary fields are equal.

\section{Proof structure}
The known-inverse equations imply
\[
  J_K(O)=\sum_{k\in K}\lVert A_kO_k-B_k\rVert_F^2=0.
\]
Every summed objective is nonnegative, while pointwise polar minimality gives
$J_K(Q)\le J_K(O)=0$. Hence $J_K(O)=J_K(Q)$. Because every local factor $H_k$ is positive
definite, equality of the summed objective with its minimum invokes the finite-mesh uniqueness
theorem and yields $O=Q$.

The proof does not infer equality of summands merely from a zero sum without sign information:
nonnegativity is proved separately from the exact squared Frobenius representation.
