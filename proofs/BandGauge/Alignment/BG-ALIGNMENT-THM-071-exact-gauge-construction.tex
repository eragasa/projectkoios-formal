\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $F_0$ be an exact finite rectangular complex frame with $F_0^*F_0=I$, let $G$ be an exact
finite complex unitary, and let $D$ be an exact represented operator. Define
\[
 F'=F_0G,\qquad H'=G^*DG.
\]
Then $F'^*F'=I$, the complex spectra of $H'$ and $D$ agree, and the supplied known inverse $G^*$
recovers both reference objects:
\[
 F'G^*=F_0,\qquad G H'G^*=D.
\]

\section{Proof structure}
Existing right-unitary frame algebra proves orthonormality preservation. Mathlib unitary similarity
proves spectrum equality. The unitary identities $GG^*=I$ and $G^*G=I$, associativity, and the
right-action composition theorem prove the two recovery identities. No numerical inverse or
optimizer is constructed.
