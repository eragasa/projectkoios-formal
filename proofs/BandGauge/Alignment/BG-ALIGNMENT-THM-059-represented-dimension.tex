\section{Exact statement and proof}
For every $r\in\mathbb N$, the canonical Euclidean right-unitary identity indexed by
\texttt{Fin r} has represented dimension $r$. The identity stores no independent dimension value:
its dimension is definitionally the finite cardinality of the retained index type. The proof reduces
the claim to $|\mathrm{Fin}\ r|=r$.
