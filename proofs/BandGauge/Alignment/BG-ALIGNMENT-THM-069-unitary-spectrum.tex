\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For any finite retained index type, exact complex unitary $G$, and exact represented operator $D$,
\[
 \operatorname{spectrum}_{\mathbb C}(G^*DG)
 =\operatorname{spectrum}_{\mathbb C}(D).
\]
The operator need not be diagonal or Hermitian. A diagonal analytic parent is a specialization.
