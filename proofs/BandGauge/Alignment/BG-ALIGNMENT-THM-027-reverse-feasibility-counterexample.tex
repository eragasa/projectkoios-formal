\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every strict inclusion of alignment families, there exists an exact acceptance predicate feasible in the larger family and infeasible in the smaller family.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
Only strict set inclusion is assumed.  The acceptance predicate is constructed by the theorem and is not claimed to be a physical loss threshold.

\section{Mathematical argument}
Strict inclusion supplies an alignment in the larger family but not the smaller.  Define acceptance to mean equality with that alignment.  The alignment itself witnesses larger-family feasibility.  Any smaller-family feasible witness satisfying equality would rewrite to the excluded alignment and contradict its nonmembership.
