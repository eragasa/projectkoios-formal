\section{Exact mathematical statement}
A two-point field taking distinct unitary values at two distinct points does not belong to the global constant-unitary family.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
Two distinct points and two unequal linear isometric equivalences are supplied; point equality is decidable.

\section{Mathematical argument}
Assume global membership.  Then the field equals one constant field.  Evaluate that equality at the selected and other points.  The two evaluation lemmas identify the resulting values with the two supplied unitaries, while constancy identifies them with each other, contradicting their inequality.
