\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For alignment families $\mathcal U_1\subseteq\mathcal U_2$, feasibility of an acceptance predicate in $\mathcal U_1$ implies feasibility in $\mathcal U_2$.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
The acceptance predicate is arbitrary.  The only structural premise is the explicit family inclusion.

\section{Mathematical argument}
Unpack feasibility in the smaller family to obtain one alignment, its smaller-family membership, and acceptance.  Apply the inclusion to the membership proof and reuse the same alignment and acceptance proof as the larger-family witness.
