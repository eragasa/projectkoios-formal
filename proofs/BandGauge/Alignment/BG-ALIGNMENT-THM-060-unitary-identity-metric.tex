\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $U$ be an exact finite complex unitary matrix. Then $U$ is unitary in the identity metric:
\[
  U^* I U = I.
\]
This is the identity-metric specialization of the separately defined predicate
$U^* M U=M$.

\section{Proof}
Matrix multiplication by the identity reduces the left side to $U^*U$. Mathlib's exact membership
condition for the finite unitary group supplies $U^*U=I$.
