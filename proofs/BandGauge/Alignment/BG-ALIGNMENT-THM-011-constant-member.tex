\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every complex linear isometric equivalence $U$ between the reference and candidate fibers,
the field that equals $U$ at every point belongs to the global-unitary alignment family.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
The unitary equivalence $U$ is supplied. The global family consists precisely of fields induced
by one equivalence common to every point. No nonempty point-domain hypothesis is needed.

\section{Mathematical argument}

field. Choose the supplied $U$ itself. The required equality is reflexive, so $U$ and
\texttt{rfl} form the complete membership witness.
