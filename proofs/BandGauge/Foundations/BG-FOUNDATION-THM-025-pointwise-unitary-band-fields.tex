\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be a finite index type. For eight real coordinate fields
$t,x,y,z,T,X,Y,Z:K\to\mathbb R$ and an exact unitary field
$U:K\to U(2)$, assume for every $k\in K$ that
\[
 H(T_k,X_k,Y_k,Z_k)=U_k^*H(t_k,x_k,y_k,z_k)U_k.
\]
Then the canonical lower-band functions are equal and the canonical upper-band functions are
equal:
\[
 \bigl(k\mapsto T_k-r(X_k,Y_k,Z_k)\bigr)
 =\bigl(k\mapsto t_k-r(x_k,y_k,z_k)\bigr),
\]
\[
 \bigl(k\mapsto T_k+r(X_k,Y_k,Z_k)\bigr)
 =\bigl(k\mapsto t_k+r(x_k,y_k,z_k)\bigr).
\]
The finite type $K$ may be a finite momentum mesh, but the theorem does not authenticate an
external mesh or use any mesh geometry.

\section{Evidence and interpretation boundaries}
Quantification over a supplied finite index type is not authentication of a runtime mesh and does not
establish mesh completeness, reciprocal geometry, periodic sewing, regularity, continuum
coverage, band tracking, or numerical accuracy. The result does not verify a computed gauge,
coordinate conversion, eigensolver, serialization, Fourier transform, truncation, or scientific
model. In particular, exact pre-truncation band equality does not imply equal finite-support
representations or equal post-truncation errors.
