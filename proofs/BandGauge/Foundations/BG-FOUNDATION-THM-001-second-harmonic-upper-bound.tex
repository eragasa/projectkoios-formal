\section{Statement and proof}
For all real $b_0,c,d,\phi$,
\[b_0+c\cos(2\phi)+d\sin(2\phi)\le b_0+\sqrt{c^2+d^2}.\]
The proof expands the square of the harmonic and its orthogonal companion, uses
$\sin^2+\cos^2=1$, and compares nonnegative squares.
