\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For real coordinates $t,x,y,z,T,X,Y,Z$, let
\[
 H(t,x,y,z)=\begin{pmatrix}t+z&x-iy\\x+iy&t-z\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad r(x,y,z)=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}.
\]
Let $U$ be an exact complex $2\times2$ unitary. If
\[
 H(T,X,Y,Z)=U^*H(t,x,y,z)U,
\]
then both canonical band values agree:
\[
 T-r(X,Y,Z)=t-r(x,y,z),\qquad
 T+r(X,Y,Z)=t+r(x,y,z).
\]

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
The unitary is supplied as an exact member of \texttt{Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin 2) $\mathbb C$}.
Both source and conjugated matrices are supplied in the same real scalar-plus-Pauli convention.
The equality with $U^*HU$ is an exact hypothesis, not a checked runtime result. The canonical
lower and upper bands are $t-r$ and $t+r$; their order was proved separately.

\section{Proof structure}
Cyclicity of trace and $UU^*=I$ prove trace invariance, while multiplicativity of determinant and
$U^*U=I$ prove determinant invariance. The coordinate formulas
\[
 \operatorname{tr}H(t,x,y,z)=2t,
 \qquad \det H(t,x,y,z)=t^2-(x^2+y^2+z^2)
\]
first force $T=t$ and then equality of the squared Pauli radii. Applying the nonnegative square root
gives equal radii. Substitution into $t-r$ and $t+r$ proves the conjunction.
