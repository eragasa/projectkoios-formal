\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $r\ge2$, let $j,k<r$, let $0\le\lambda\le1$, and let $B$ be a nonempty finite bond inventory.
For exact phases $\theta_b$, define
\[
 c_B=\frac1{|B|}\sum_{b\in B}\left(\frac34\cos\theta_b+
 \frac14\cos(2\theta_b)\right),\qquad
 \xi_j=\frac{2j-r+1}{r-1},
\]
\[
 e^-_j=\frac25\xi_j+\frac14\lambda\left(1+\frac15\xi_j\right)c_B,
 \qquad e^+_k=3+\frac3{10}\xi_k+\frac1{10}\lambda c_B.
\]
Then
\[
 e^+_k-e^-_j\ge\frac{19}{10}.
\]

\section{Proof structure}
The standard cosine bounds imply every weighted bond harmonic lies in $[-1,1]$. A finite nonempty
equal-weight average therefore gives $c_B\in[-1,1]$. The selected orbital coordinates also lie in
$[-1,1]$. Exact real algebra then gives $e^-_j\le7/10$ and $e^+_k\ge13/5$; subtraction yields
$13/5-7/10=19/10$.
