\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Under the same nonempty finite-bond structure factor and parent-scale assumptions as
\texttt{BG-FOUNDATION-THM-035}, for $r\ge2$ and every adjacent coordinate pair,
\[
 e^-_{j+1}-e^-_j\ge\frac{7}{10(r-1)}>0.
\]
The theorem is algebraic for every natural $j$. The companion
\texttt{BG-FOUNDATION-THM-037} supplies $j+1<r$ and concludes the strict selected-index ordering
$e^-_j<e^-_{j+1}$.

\section{Proof structure}
Adjacent orbital coordinates satisfy
\[
 \xi_{j+1}-\xi_j=\frac{2}{r-1}.
\]
Retained-energy subtraction factors exactly as
\[
 e^-(\xi_{j+1})-e^-(\xi_j)
 =\frac{2}{r-1}\left(\frac25+\frac1{20}\lambda c_B\right).
\]
Since $\lambda c_B\ge-1$, the coefficient is at least $7/20$. Multiplication by the positive
factor $2/(r-1)$ yields the declared lower bound.
