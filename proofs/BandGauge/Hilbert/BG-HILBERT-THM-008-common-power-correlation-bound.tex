\section{Exact statement}
For nonnegative coordinate weights and $E_{\mathrm{ref}}\ne0$, suppose two finite
rank-two matrix fields satisfy $P(X)=\kappa$ and $P(Y)=\kappa$. Then
\[
 |C(X,Y)|\le\kappa.
\]

\section{Source and relationship}
This theorem \texttt{formalizes} the Cauchy--Schwarz step needed for the Paper A bound
$|b_\phi|\le\kappa$, using the definitions near lines 606--625 of the v3 manuscript in

\begin{center}\small\texttt{99a46cb59d0c6f27ada7d709f194996ef9619d9f}.\end{center}
BG-HILBERT-THM-007 supplies the common-power equalities when both fields are pointwise
unitary conjugates of one parent field.

\section{Proof and boundary}
BG-HILBERT-THM-006 gives $|C(X,Y)|\le\sqrt{P(X)}\sqrt{P(Y)}$. Substituting the common
power gives $\sqrt\kappa\sqrt\kappa=\kappa$; nonnegativity of $\kappa$ follows from
BG-HILBERT-THM-005.

The theorem does not identify $X$ and $Y$ with the source's concrete $D_{i,\phi}$ and
$B_i$, derive the scalar loss polynomial, or validate numerical data. Lean and pinned
Mathlib report \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
No admission or scientific axiom is used. The proof and prose were AI-assisted and
kernel-checked. No independent expert review is recorded. Status: \texttt{proved};
