\section{Exact statement}
For nonnegative radii there exist $x\in B(c_1,r_1)$ and $y\in B(c_2,r_2)$ satisfying
\[
 d(x,y)=\max\{0,d(c_1,c_2)-r_1-r_2\}.
\]

\section{Proof}
If the balls intersect, a common point attains distance zero. Otherwise let
$d=d(c_1,c_2)>r_1+r_2$. On the center line choose parameters $r_1/d$ and $1-r_2/d$.
The resulting points lie on the respective ball boundaries, and their parameter gap
times $d$ is $d-r_1-r_2$, the positive branch of the formula.
