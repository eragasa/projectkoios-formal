\section{Exact statement}
For a finite rank-two matrix field $X$, nonnegative weights $\alpha_i$, and
$E_{\mathrm{ref}}\ne0$,
\[
 0\le \sum_i\frac{\alpha_i}{2E_{\mathrm{ref}}^2}
       \sum_{m,n}|X_i(m,n)|^2.
\]

\section{Source and relationship}

The source assumes strictly positive normalized weights and positive reference energy;
the theorem uses only the weaker assumptions required by the inequality.

\section{Proof and boundary}
Scale every matrix entry by the square root of its nonnegative coefficient and place the
entries in a finite complex Euclidean space. The displayed sum is the squared norm of
that vector, hence is nonnegative. This does not identify $X$ with a scientific dataset
or derive the paper's complete operator-loss polynomial.
