\section{Exact statement}
Let $d_{in}$ be a finite real two-state field with $\sum_n d_{in}=0$ at each
coordinate, let $\sum_i\alpha_i=1$, and let $E_{\mathrm{ref}}\ne0$. The weighted
squared residual associated with
\[
 E_{\mathrm{ref}}s+(\lambda-1)d_{in}
\]
is exactly
\[
 s^2+(\lambda-1)^2P(d),
\qquad
 P(d)=\sum_{i,n}\frac{\alpha_i}{2E_{\mathrm{ref}}^2}d_{in}^2.
\]

\section{Source and proof}
This theorem \texttt{formalizes} the spectral-channel expansion in Supplementary
Section S1.1 of Ragasa2026A. Owner: \path{ksdft2effmass}; immutable commit
\begin{center}\small\texttt{99a46cb59d0c6f27ada7d709f194996ef9619d9f}.\end{center}
Source: \path{docs/publications/papers/Ragasa2026a/v3/}\path{supplementary.tex},
lines 51--68. Real polarization expands the residual. Two-state centering cancels the
cross term. Rank two and normalized weights reduce the constant contribution to $s^2$.
