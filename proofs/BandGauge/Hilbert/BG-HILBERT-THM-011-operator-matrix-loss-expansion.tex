\section{Exact statement}
Let $D_i$ and $B_i$ be pointwise traceless complex $2\times2$ matrices, let
$\sum_i\alpha_i=1$, and let $E_{\mathrm{ref}}\ne0$. For the residual
$E_{\mathrm{ref}}sI+\lambda D_i-B_i$, its weighted squared Frobenius loss is
\[
 s^2+\lambda^2P(D)-2\lambda C(D,B)+P(B).
\]

shift plus that tail. Tracelessness removes the shift cross term. Rank two, normalized
weights, and the source coefficient reduce the identity contribution to $s^2$.
