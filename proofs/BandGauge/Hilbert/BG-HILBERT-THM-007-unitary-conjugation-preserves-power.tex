\section{Exact statement}
For a finite rank-two matrix field $X_i$ and arbitrary unitary matrices $U_i$,
\[
 P\bigl(i\mapsto U_i^\dagger X_iU_i\bigr)=P(X),
\]
where $P$ is the source-normalized weighted Frobenius power. This algebraic equality does
not require signs on the scalar coefficients.

\section{Proof and boundary}
For each coordinate, express squared Frobenius norm as
$\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(A^\dagger A)$. Expand the conjugated product, cancel
$U_iU_i^\dagger=I$, and cyclically move the remaining unitary factors through the trace.
Thus every coordinate power is unchanged, so the weighted sum is unchanged.

This proves algebraic invariance only; it does not validate any computed alignment or
source matrix. Lean and pinned Mathlib report \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}. No admission or scientific axiom is
used. The proof and prose were AI-assisted and kernel-checked. No independent expert
review is recorded. Status: \texttt{proved}; working draft.
