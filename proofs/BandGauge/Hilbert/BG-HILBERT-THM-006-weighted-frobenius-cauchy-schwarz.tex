\section{Exact statement}
Write $P(X)$ for the source-normalized weighted Frobenius power and
\[
 C(X,Y)=\sum_i\frac{\alpha_i}{2E_{\mathrm{ref}}^2}
   \sum_{m,n}\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\overline{X_i(m,n)}Y_i(m,n)\right).
\]
For nonnegative weights and $E_{\mathrm{ref}}\ne0$,
\[
 |C(X,Y)|\le\sqrt{P(X)}\sqrt{P(Y)}.
\]

\section{Proof and boundary}
Embed both fields in the finite complex Euclidean space obtained by multiplying each
entry by the square root of its weight coefficient. The correlation is the real part of
the complex inner product. Its absolute value is bounded first by the complex norm and
then by Cauchy--Schwarz. The two vector norms are the square roots of $P(X)$ and $P(Y)$.
No bounded-operator norm or continuum section space is used.
