\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every real $z$,
\[
  \int_0^{2\pi} \exp\!\bigl(i z\cos\theta\bigr)\,d\theta
   = 2\pi\,J_0(z),
\]
where $J_0$ is Mathlib's \texttt{Complex.besselJ 0}. The parameter is real and the equality is in
$\mathbb C$.

\section{Proof structure}
Define $M_n=\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^n\theta\,d\theta$. The standard two-step recurrence gives
$M_{2n}=2\pi(2n)!/(4^n(n!)^2)$ and $M_{2n+1}=0$. Expand the exponential as its norm-convergent
factorial series. A summable majorant $|z|^n/n!$ justifies termwise interval integration. Odd terms
vanish, while each even term equals $2\pi$ times the corresponding coefficient in Mathlib's
regularized-hypergeometric definition of $J_0$. Summing yields the claimed identity.
