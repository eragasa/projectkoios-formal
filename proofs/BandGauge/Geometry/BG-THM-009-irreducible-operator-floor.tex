\section{Exact mathematical statement}

For $p=(s,\lambda)$ in the Euclidean parameter plane, define
\[
 L_{\mathrm{op},\mu}^2(p)
 =s^2+\kappa(\lambda-\mu)^2+\kappa(1-\mu^2).
\]
If $\kappa\ge0$, then
\[
 \kappa(1-\mu^2)\le L_{\mathrm{op},\mu}^2(p).
\]

\section{Human-readable proof}

Both $s^2$ and $(\lambda-\mu)^2$ are nonnegative. Since $\kappa\ge0$, the term
$\kappa(\lambda-\mu)^2$ is also nonnegative. Adding these two terms to
$\kappa(1-\mu^2)$ cannot decrease it, giving the claimed lower bound.
