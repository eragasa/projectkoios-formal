\section{Exact mathematical statement}

Let $X$ be one metric candidate-parameter domain. Let $L_E,L_H:X\to\mathbb R$, with
budgets $\tau_E,\tau_H$. If
\[
 0<\ell\le\delta_{\mathrm e}(\mathcal A_E,\mathcal A_H),
\]
where the two admissible sets are the corresponding loss sublevel sets, then
\[
 \neg\exists\theta\in X:\quad
 L_E(\theta)\le\tau_E\ \land\ L_H(\theta)\le\tau_H.
\]

\section{Human-readable proof}

BG-THM-003 converts the positive certified lower bound into disjointness of the two
admissible sets. Assume a candidate satisfies both loss bounds. By the definitions of the
sublevel sets, that candidate belongs to both sets, contradicting disjointness. Therefore
no candidate satisfies both budgets.
