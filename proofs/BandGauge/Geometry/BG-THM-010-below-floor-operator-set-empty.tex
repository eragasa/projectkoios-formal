\section{Exact mathematical statement}

Let
\[
 \mathcal A_o=
 \{p:L_{\mathrm{op},\mu}^2(p)\le\tau_o^2\}.
\]
If $\kappa\ge0$ and
\[
 \tau_o^2<\kappa(1-\mu^2),
\]
then $\mathcal A_o=\varnothing$.

\section{Human-readable proof}

Assume a point belongs to the operator admissible set. Its squared loss is then at most
$\tau_o^2$. BG-THM-009 says the same loss is at least $\kappa(1-\mu^2)$. Together these
inequalities contradict $\tau_o^2<\kappa(1-\mu^2)$. No admissible point exists.
