\section{Exact mathematical statement}

Let $X$ be a metric space, let $A,B\subseteq X$, and define
\[
 \delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)
 =\inf_{a\in A,\,b\in B} d_{\mathrm e}(a,b)
 \in[0,\infty].
\]
If $\theta\in A\cap B$, then
\[
 \delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)=0.
\]

For the paper's decision problem, $A=\mathcal A_E$ is the spectral admissible set and
$B=\mathcal A_H$ is the aligned-operator admissible set in one declared candidate
model-parameter domain. The same candidate $\theta$ meeting both error budgets is the
common witness or joint match.

$\Downarrow$ \\
$(\theta,\theta)$ is a feasible pair \\
$\Downarrow$ \\
$\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)\le d_{\mathrm e}(\theta,\theta)=0$ \\
$\Downarrow\quad$ since $0\le\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)$ \\
$\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)=0$

\section{Human-readable proof}

The intersection hypothesis gives both $\theta\in A$ and $\theta\in B$. Apply the
feasible-pair upper-bound theorem to the pair $(\theta,\theta)$. Its self-distance is
zero, so $\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)\le0$. Extended distances are nonnegative, hence
$0\le\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)$. Antisymmetry gives
$\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)=0$.
