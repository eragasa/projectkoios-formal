\section{Exact mathematical statement}

Let $X$ be one metric candidate-parameter domain. Let $L_E,L_H:X\to\mathbb R$ and let
$\tau_E,\tau_H\in\mathbb R$. Define
\[
 \mathcal A_E=\{\theta:L_E(\theta)\le\tau_E\},\qquad
 \mathcal A_H=\{\theta:L_H(\theta)\le\tau_H\}.
\]
If one candidate $\theta$ satisfies both loss bounds, then
\[
 \delta_{\mathrm e}(\mathcal A_E,\mathcal A_H)=0.
\]
