\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a metric space and let $A,B\subseteq X$ be nonempty compact sets. Then
there exist $a\in A$ and $b\in B$ such that
\[
  \delta_{\rm e}(A,B)=d_{\rm e}(a,b),
\]
where $\delta_{\rm e}$ is the extended infimum used by the repository. Disjointness is
not required for attainment.

\section{Assumptions and proof}
Compactness and nonemptiness are explicit. The product $A\times B$ is nonempty and
compact, and continuous extended distance attains a minimum there. One inequality is the
defining infimum bound at the minimizing pair. The reverse inequality is the pair's
minimality against every $(a',b')\in A\times B$. Antisymmetry identifies the values.
