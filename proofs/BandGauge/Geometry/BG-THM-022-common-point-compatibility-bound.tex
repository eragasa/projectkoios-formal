\section{Exact statement}
For $\kappa>0$, $\tau_s\ge0$, and $r\ge0$, membership of one point in both full ellipses
implies
\[
 \sqrt{\kappa}\,|1-\mu|\le\tau_s+r.
\]

\section{Proof}
Dropping the nonnegative $s^2$ term from each ellipse inequality and taking nonnegative
square roots gives
$\sqrt\kappa|\lambda-1|\le\tau_s$ and
$\sqrt\kappa|\lambda-\mu|\le r$. The triangle inequality for
$1-\mu=(1-\lambda)+(\lambda-\mu)$ gives the result.
