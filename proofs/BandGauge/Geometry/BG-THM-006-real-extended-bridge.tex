\section{Exact mathematical statement}

For nonempty subsets $A,B$ of a metric space, define
\[
 \delta(A,B)=\operatorname{toReal}(\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)).
\]
Then
\[
 \operatorname{ofReal}(\delta(A,B))=\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B).
\]

\section{Human-readable proof}

BG-THM-005 proves that the extended distance is finite. For every finite extended
nonnegative real, converting to a real number and then applying `ofReal` returns the
original value. Applying that library identity to $\delta_{\mathrm e}(A,B)$ proves the
claim.
