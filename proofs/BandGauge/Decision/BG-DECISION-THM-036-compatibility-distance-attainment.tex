\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Assume the common candidate carrier is metric and both central admissible sets for a
model are nonempty. Then there are a spectral-admissible candidate $c_E$ and an
operator-admissible candidate $c_H$ with
\[
  \operatorname{compatibilityEDist}(j)=d_{\rm e}(c_E,c_H).
\]
The two candidates need not coincide.
\section{Provenance and proof argument}

application of compact distance attainment. No optimizer or enumeration algorithm is
