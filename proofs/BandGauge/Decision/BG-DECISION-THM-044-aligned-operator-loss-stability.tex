\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For one supplied alignment $U$ and two reference/candidate pairs $(R_L,C_L)$ and $(R_R,C_R)$,
\[
 L_U(R_L,C_L)\leq L_U(R_R,C_R)
 +\lVert R_L-R_R\rVert_\infty+\lVert C_L-C_R\rVert_\infty.
\]
Every field norm is the finite maximum of exact continuous-linear-map operator norms.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Let $\Delta_L=R_L-U^{-1}C_LU$ and $\Delta_R=R_R-U^{-1}C_RU$.  The reverse triangle estimate gives
\[
 \lVert\Delta_L\rVert_\infty\leq
 \lVert\Delta_R\rVert_\infty+\lVert\Delta_L-\Delta_R\rVert_\infty.
\]
The finite lift of \texttt{BG-BLOCH-THM-031} bounds the last term by the two input-field changes.  Coercion of nonnegative norms into extended nonnegative reals preserves addition and order.
