\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a finite evaluation type $K$, exact operator fields $R_k,C_k$, and one supplied pointwise unitary alignment $U_k$, define
\[
 L_U(R,C)=\left\|k\mapsto R_k-U_k^{-1}C_kU_k\right\|_{\infty}.
\]
Then
\[
 L_U(R,C)=\sup_{k\in K}\left\|R_k-U_k^{-1}C_kU_k\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Lean records both sides in the extended nonnegative reals.  The finite supremum is zero for an empty inventory.
