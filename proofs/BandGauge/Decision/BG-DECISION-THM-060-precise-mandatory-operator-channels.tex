\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Fix one model $m$, candidate $c$, and alignment $a$.  The precise channel data contain a finite error-channel inventory $E_m$ and a finite structural-requirement inventory $R_m$.  Every $e\in E_m$ has an exact value $v(m,c,a,e)\in[0,\infty]$ and its own exact upper budget $b(m,e)$.  Every $r\in R_m$ has a supplied proposition $P(m,c,a,r)$.  After the tagged adapter replaces the generic channel fields, generic alignment-channel acceptance is equivalent to
\[
  \bigl(\forall e\in E_m,\ v(m,c,a,e)\le b(m,e)\bigr)
  \quad\land\quad
  \bigl(\forall r\in R_m,\ P(m,c,a,r)\bigr).
\]
The same $m$, $c$, and $a$ occur in every obligation.
