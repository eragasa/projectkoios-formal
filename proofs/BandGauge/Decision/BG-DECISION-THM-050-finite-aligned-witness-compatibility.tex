\section{Exact mathematical statement}
A model $m$ is witness-compatible in the adapted schema if there exist one candidate $c$ and one alignment-carrier value $a$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $c$ belongs to the declared finite candidate inventory;
\item its exact spectral loss is within the declared spectral budget;
\item $a$ belongs to the declared family for $(m,c)$;
\item the concrete finite aligned-operator loss projected from $a$ is within the operator budget; and
\item every mandatory channel accepts the same $(m,c,a)$.
\end{enumerate}
The theorem proves the forward construction from these five explicit premises.
