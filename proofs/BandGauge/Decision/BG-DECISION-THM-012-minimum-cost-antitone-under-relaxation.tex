\section{Statement}
Given minimum-cost decisions for strict and relaxed coordinatewise budgets, the relaxed
minimum cost is no larger than the strict minimum cost.
\section{Argument}
The strict minimizer remains feasible after threshold relaxation by
BG-DECISION-THM-010. Minimality of the relaxed minimizer therefore bounds its cost above
by the strict minimizer's cost.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
Both minimizers are explicit hypotheses, so the theorem does not hide empty-feasibility
behavior. It does not authorize post hoc threshold changes. Lean checked the exact
statement; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
