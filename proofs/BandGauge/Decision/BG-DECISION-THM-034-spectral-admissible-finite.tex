\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every model in a supplied finite compatibility schema, the set of candidates that
belong to the declared candidate inventory and satisfy the spectral budget is finite.
\section{Provenance and proof argument}
The theorem supports the finite separation converse requested by the Formal proof
companion at merge \texttt{1078380fb608269a8094c7e86776a1c0d826333a}. By definition,
every spectral-admissible candidate carries a proof of membership in the model's
\texttt{Finset} candidate inventory. The admissible set is therefore a subset of the
finite coercion of that inventory, and finiteness follows by subset closure. This is
source-neutral finite-set logic, not a claim that the inventory is scientifically
complete.
