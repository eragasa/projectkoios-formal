\section{Statement}
If $a$ weakly improves $b$ and $b$ weakly improves $c$, then $a$ weakly improves $c$.
\section{Argument}
For every objective $j$, the hypotheses give $f(a,j)\le f(b,j)$ and
$f(b,j)\le f(c,j)$. Transitivity gives $f(a,j)\le f(c,j)$.
