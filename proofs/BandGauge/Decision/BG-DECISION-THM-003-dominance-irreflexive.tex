\section{Statement}
Under weak-all/strict-any minimization dominance, no decision dominates itself.
\section{Argument}
Self-dominance would require an objective $j$ satisfying $f(d,j)<f(d,j)$, contradicting
irreflexivity of strict order in a preorder.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
This theorem fixes the declared dominance convention. It says nothing about the
correctness or completeness of a computational objective table. Lean checked the exact
statement; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
