\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Fix a finite compatibility schema, model $m$, model-indexed four-stage residual fields, and exact
four-stage budgets with total headroom at $m$. After replacing only the schema's mandatory-channel
fields by the narrow four-stage adapter, witness compatibility is equivalent to the existence of
one candidate $c$ and one alignment $a$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $c$ passes the original schema's spectral criterion at $m$;
\item $c$ belongs to the original candidate inventory;
\item $a$ belongs to the original alignment family for $(m,c)$;
\item the original aligned-operator loss at $(m,c,a)$ lies below the original operator budget; and
\item at the same $(m,c,a)$, interpolation, conversion, and deliberate-truncation finite-maximum
residual values lie below their respective exact component budgets.
\end{enumerate}
The total residual channel passes by the separate total-headroom theorem.
