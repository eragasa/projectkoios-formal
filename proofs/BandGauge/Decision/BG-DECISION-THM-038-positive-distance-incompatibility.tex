\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a metric candidate carrier and a model whose central spectral and operator admissible
sets are both nonempty,
\[
  0<\operatorname{compatibilityEDist}(j)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \neg\operatorname{Compatible}(j).
\]
\section{Provenance and proof argument}
This is the finite-inventory negative counterpart selected by the Formal proof companion
at merge \texttt{1078380fb608269a8094c7e86776a1c0d826333a}. Finiteness supplies compactness.
The compact positive-distance theorem converts positivity to disjointness. Disjointness
of the spectral and operator admissible sets is equivalent to failure of their nonempty
intersection, which is exactly failure of central compatibility.
