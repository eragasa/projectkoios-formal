\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Fix model $m$, candidate $c$, and alignment-carrier value $a$.  If
\begin{enumerate}
\item $a$ belongs to the declared alignment family for $(m,c)$;
\item the exact finite aligned-operator loss obtained from the projected unitary field of $a$ is at most the operator budget for $m$; and
\item every mandatory operator channel accepts the same $(m,c,a)$,
\end{enumerate}
then $a$ is a \texttt{ConformantOperatorWitness} for the adapted generic compatibility schema.
