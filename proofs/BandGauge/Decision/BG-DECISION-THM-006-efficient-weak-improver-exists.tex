\section{Statement}
For a finite decision type and preorder-valued objective table, every decision $d$ is
weakly improved by some Pareto-efficient decision $e$.
\section{Argument}
Consider the nonempty set $S$ of decisions weakly improving $d$; it contains $d$.
Dominance is transitive and irreflexive, hence well founded on the finite decision type.
Choose a dominance-minimal $e\in S$. If another decision dominated $e$, transitivity of
weak improvement would put that decision in $S$, contradicting minimality. Thus $e$ is
globally efficient and weakly improves $d$.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
Finiteness applies only to the declared decision type. The result does not interpolate,
convexify, or establish a continuum Pareto frontier. Lean checked the statement with the
pinned toolchain; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
