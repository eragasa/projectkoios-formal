\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a metric candidate carrier and a model whose central spectral and operator admissible
sets are both nonempty,
\[
  \operatorname{compatibilityEDist}(j)=0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \operatorname{Compatible}(j).
\]
Compatibility remains literal intersection through one common candidate.
\section{Provenance and proof argument}
The proof companion at merge
\texttt{1078380fb608269a8094c7e86776a1c0d826333a} explicitly withheld this converse until
attainment, compactness, or direct finite geometry was proved. Both central sets are
finite subsets of the declared candidate inventory, hence compact. The compact
zero-distance/intersection theorem then applies, and unfolding \texttt{Compatible}
identifies nonempty intersection with central compatibility.
