\section{Statement}
For any objective table $f:D\to J\to V$ valued in a preorder and any $d\in D$,
$d$ weakly improves itself: $f(d,j)\le f(d,j)$ for every $j\in J$.
\section{Argument}
Fix an arbitrary objective $j$. Reflexivity of the preorder gives
$f(d,j)\le f(d,j)$. Since $j$ was arbitrary, the coordinatewise relation holds.
\section{Boundary and provenance}

or publication claim. Lean checked the exact statement with the pinned toolchain. The
dossier and proof were AI-assisted and remain subject to source and expert review.
