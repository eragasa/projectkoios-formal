\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $D_1$ and $D_2$ be precise mandatory-channel datasets on the same model, candidate, and
alignment carriers. Their error identifiers are tagged by $E_1\sqcup E_2$, and their structural
identifiers by $S_1\sqcup S_2$. For every common model $m$, candidate $c$, and alignment $a$,
\[
 \operatorname{Accepts}(D_1\mathbin{\oplus}D_2;m,c,a)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Accepts}(D_1;m,c,a)\ \land\
 \operatorname{Accepts}(D_2;m,c,a).
\]
Each original error inequality and each original nonnumeric structural proposition remains
mandatory at the same $(m,c,a)$.

\section{Proof structure}
The combined error inventory is the disjoint sum of the original error inventories, and constructor
case analysis dispatches values and budgets to the corresponding input. Thus combined error
acceptance is equivalent to both original error acceptances. The same argument applies independently
to the disjoint structural inventories and predicates. Expanding precise acceptance into its error
and structural conjuncts, applying both equivalences, and reassociating conjunction proves the
statement.
