\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Fix one model class $m$ and one candidate $c$.  Let $A_{m,c}$ be its declared set of alignments and let $L_{m,c}:A\to[0,\infty]$ be the exact aligned-operator loss.  If $A_{m,c}$ is finite and nonempty, then there exists $a_*\in A_{m,c}$ such that
\[
  L_{m,c}(a_*)\le L_{m,c}(a)\qquad\text{for every }a\in A_{m,c}.
\]
Thus $a_*$ satisfies the existing \texttt{AttainsAlignedOperatorLoss} predicate.
