\section{Statement}
If $a$ weakly improves a threshold-feasible decision $b$, then $a$ is feasible for the
same coordinatewise budget vector.
\section{Argument}
For each objective $j$, weak improvement gives $f(a,j)\le f(b,j)$ and feasibility gives
$f(b,j)\le\tau(j)$. Transitivity gives $f(a,j)\le\tau(j)$.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
The same decision is used in every objective. The theorem does not permit componentwise
envelopes assembled from different decisions. Lean checked the exact statement; the
dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
