\section{Statement}
A finite inventory contains a threshold-feasible decision if and only if it contains a
Pareto-efficient threshold-feasible decision.
\section{Argument}
The forward implication is BG-DECISION-THM-008. The reverse implication forgets the
efficiency conjunct and retains the same feasible witness.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
This justifies searching an exactly computed efficient subset for coordinatewise
feasibility. It does not justify an approximate mask, interpolated surface, or incomplete
inventory. Lean checked the exact statement; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
