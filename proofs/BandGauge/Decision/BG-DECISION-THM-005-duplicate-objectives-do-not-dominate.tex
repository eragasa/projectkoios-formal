\section{Statement}
If two decisions have equal values in every objective, the first does not dominate the
second under weak-all/strict-any dominance.
\section{Argument}
Dominance would supply an objective with a strict inequality. Substitution of the
coordinatewise equality turns it into $x<x$, a contradiction.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
The theorem deliberately permits distinct decisions with duplicate objective vectors to
remain efficient. Deduplication is a separate data policy. Lean checked the exact
statement; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
