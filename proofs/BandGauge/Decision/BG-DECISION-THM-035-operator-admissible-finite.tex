\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every model in a supplied finite compatibility schema, the set of candidates that
belong to the declared candidate inventory, have a nonempty alignment family, and pass
the aligned-operator infimum budget is finite.
\section{Provenance and proof argument}
The Formal proof companion at merge
\texttt{1078380fb608269a8094c7e86776a1c0d826333a} identifies finite-set compactness as the
route to exact separation converses. Operator acceptance includes candidate-inventory
membership as its first conjunct. Hence the operator-admissible set is a subset of the
finite inventory coercion and is finite. The argument does not inspect or attain the
alignment infimum.
