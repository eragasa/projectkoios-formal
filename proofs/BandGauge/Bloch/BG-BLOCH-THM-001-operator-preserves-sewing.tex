\section{Exact statement}
Let a group $\Gamma$ act on momentum labels $K$, and let $S(g,k)$ be unitary fiber
transport. Suppose
\[
 f(g\cdot k)=S(g,k)f(k)
\]
and
\[
 A(g\cdot k)S(g,k)v=S(g,k)A(k)v.
\]
Then $k\mapsto A(k)f(k)$ obeys the same sewing law.

\section{Proof}
At $g\cdot k$, replace $f(g\cdot k)$ by $S(g,k)f(k)$. Operator covariance then rewrites
$A(g\cdot k)S(g,k)f(k)$ as $S(g,k)A(k)f(k)$, which is the required sewing law.
