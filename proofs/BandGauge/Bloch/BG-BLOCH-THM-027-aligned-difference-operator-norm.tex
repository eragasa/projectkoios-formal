\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a supplied pointwise unitary alignment $U_k$, reference operator $R_k$, and candidate operator $C_k$, define
\[
 \Delta_k=R_k-U_k^{-1}C_kU_k.
\]
Then the exact continuous-linear-map norms satisfy
\[
 \lVert\Delta_k\rVert\leq \lVert R_k\rVert+\lVert C_k\rVert.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
For every $v$, the action-level triangle inequality gives
\[
 \lVert\Delta_kv\rVert\leq\lVert R_kv\rVert+\lVert C_kU_kv\rVert.
\]
Each term is bounded by its operator norm times the vector norm, and $\lVert U_kv\rVert=\lVert v\rVert$.  Therefore
\[
 \lVert\Delta_kv\rVert\leq(\lVert R_k\rVert+\lVert C_k\rVert)\lVert v\rVert.
\]
The defining upper-bound property of the operator norm yields the result.
