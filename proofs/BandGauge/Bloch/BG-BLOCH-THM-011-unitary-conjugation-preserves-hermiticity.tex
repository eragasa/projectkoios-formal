\section{Statement and proof}
For a pointwise Hermitian field $D_i$ and unitary field $U_i$, every
$U_i^\dagger D_iU_i$ is Hermitian. The proof reverses products under conjugate transpose
and uses $D_i^\dagger=D_i$.
