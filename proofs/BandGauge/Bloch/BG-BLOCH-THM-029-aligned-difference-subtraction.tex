\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For one supplied alignment $U$ and two pairs of reference and candidate fields, write
$\Delta_U(R,C)=R-U^{-1}CU$.  Then the exact field identity is
\[
 \Delta_U(R_L,C_L)-\Delta_U(R_R,C_R)
 =\Delta_U(R_L-R_R,C_L-C_R).
\]

\section{Assumptions, conventions, and statement delta}
All four fields use the same supplied pointwise unitary alignment.  This common-alignment hypothesis is essential to the statement: the theorem does not compare values produced under two different gauges.  Subtraction always follows the declared reference-minus-pulled-back-candidate convention.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
At each $k$ and vector $v$, expand both aligned differences.  Linearity of $U_k^{-1}$ gives
\[
 U_k^{-1}(C_{L,k}-C_{R,k})U_kv
 =U_k^{-1}C_{L,k}U_kv-U_k^{-1}C_{R,k}U_kv.
\]
The remaining equality is an additive-group rearrangement.  Pointwise extensionality then gives equality of operator fields.
