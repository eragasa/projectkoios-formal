\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $E_{\rm ref}$ and $E_{\rm cand}$ be complete complex inner-product spaces.  For each $k\in K$, let $U_k:E_{\rm ref}\simeq E_{\rm cand}$ be a linear isometric equivalence and let $C_k$ be self-adjoint on $E_{\rm cand}$.  Then
\[
  U_k^{-1}C_kU_k
\]
is self-adjoint on $E_{\rm ref}$ for every $k$.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
For fixed $k$, the adjoint of a unitary pullback satisfies
\[
 (U_k^{-1}C_kU_k)^*=U_k^{-1}C_k^*U_k.
\]
Since $C_k^*=C_k$, the right side is the original pullback.  Lean rewrites the adjoint of the isometry as its inverse and applies Mathlib's self-adjoint conjugation lemma.
