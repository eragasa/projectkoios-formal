\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be any index type and let $E$ be a complete complex inner-product space.  For bounded complex-linear operator fields $R,C:K\to\mathcal L(E)$, if $R_k$ and $C_k$ are self-adjoint for every $k$, then
\[
  (R-C)_k=R_k-C_k\quad\text{is self-adjoint for every }k.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Fix $k$.  The hypotheses give $R_k^*=R_k$ and $C_k^*=C_k$.  Additivity of the adjoint gives
\[
 (R_k-C_k)^*=R_k^*-C_k^*=R_k-C_k.
\]
Thus the difference is self-adjoint at every index.  Lean invokes \texttt{IsSelfAdjoint.sub} pointwise.
