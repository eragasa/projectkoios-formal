\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For one supplied alignment $U_k$, define $\Delta_{L,k}=R_{L,k}-U_k^{-1}C_{L,k}U_k$ and $\Delta_{R,k}=R_{R,k}-U_k^{-1}C_{R,k}U_k$.  Then
\[
 \lVert\Delta_{L,k}-\Delta_{R,k}\rVert
 \leq \lVert R_{L,k}-R_{R,k}\rVert
      +\lVert C_{L,k}-C_{R,k}\rVert.
\]
All norms are exact continuous-linear-map operator norms.

\[
 \Delta_U(R_L,C_L)-\Delta_U(R_R,C_R)
 =\Delta_U(R_L-R_R,C_L-C_R).

Apply \texttt{BG-BLOCH-THM-027} to this new aligned difference.  Its reference and candidate operands are exactly the two field changes on the right-hand side, yielding the claimed bound.
