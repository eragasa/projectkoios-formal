\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Adding the candidate field to the reference-minus-candidate difference recovers the reference
field exactly:
\[
  \operatorname{Diff}(H_{\mathrm{ref}},H_{\mathrm{cand}})
  +H_{\mathrm{cand}}=H_{\mathrm{ref}}.
\]

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
Both fields act on the same complex fiber. No sewing, norm, self-adjointness, or invertibility
premise is required.

\section{Mathematical argument}
The identity is pointwise $(a-b)+b=a$ in the additive group of continuous linear maps. Apply it
at every point and use function extensionality to conclude equality of the operator fields.
