\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For every point $k$ and reference-fiber vector $v$, the aligned difference is
\[
 \Delta_C(k)v
 =H_{\mathrm{ref}}(k)v
  -C(k)^{-1}\bigl(H_{\mathrm{cand}}(k)(C(k)v)\bigr).
\]
The operand order is reference minus pulled-back candidate.

\section{Mathematical argument}

Expanding the aligned candidate applies the forward alignment, then the candidate operator, then the inverse alignment.  Continuous-linear-map subtraction evaluates pointwise, giving the displayed identity by definitional reduction.
