\section{Exact statement}
Let $S_r$ and $S_c$ be reference and candidate sewing systems. Let a pointwise unitary
$U(k)$ intertwine them:
\[
 U(g\cdot k)S_r(g,k)=S_c(g,k)U(k).
\]
If the candidate operator field $A_c$ is sewing-covariant, then the pulled-back field
\[
 \widetilde A(k)=U(k)^{-1}A_c(k)U(k)
\]
is covariant under $S_r$.

\section{Proof}
Apply $U(g\cdot k)$ to both sides of the desired covariance equation. The inverse in the
aligned field cancels. Sewing compatibility moves $U$ through the reference transport,
and candidate operator covariance moves $A_c$ through the candidate transport. Applying
sewing compatibility once more to the output makes both sides identical. Injectivity of
the unitary alignment proves the original equality.
