\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For operator fields $H_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and $H_{\mathrm{cand}}$ on one fiber space, every
point $k$, and every vector $v$,
\[
  \bigl(H_{\mathrm{ref}}-H_{\mathrm{cand}}\bigr)(k)v
  =H_{\mathrm{ref}}(k)v-H_{\mathrm{cand}}(k)v,
\]
where the left side uses the declared pointwise operator-field difference.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
Both fields act on the same complex inner-product-space fiber. The operand order is
reference minus candidate. No sewing, self-adjointness, finiteness, or alignment premise is used.

\section{Mathematical argument}
The operator-field difference is defined pointwise by subtracting the two continuous linear
maps at $k$. Continuous-linear-map subtraction is itself evaluated pointwise on $v$. Expanding
these two definitions yields the displayed equality without an auxiliary estimate.
