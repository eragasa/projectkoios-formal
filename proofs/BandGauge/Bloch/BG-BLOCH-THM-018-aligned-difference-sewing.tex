\section{Exact mathematical statement}
If the reference and candidate operator fields obey their respective sewing laws and the alignment intertwines the two sewing transports, then the reference-minus-pulled-back-candidate difference obeys the reference sewing law.

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
The two sewing data, the pointwise unitary alignment, and all three covariance hypotheses are explicit.  The theorem does not infer any of them from sampled arrays or endpoint matching.

\section{Mathematical argument}
The alignment covariance theorem first transports the candidate field to the reference fibers and proves that this pulled-back field obeys the reference sewing law.  Sewing covariance is closed under subtraction because the sewing transport is linear and therefore maps a difference to the difference of the transported values.  Applying that closure theorem to the reference field and pulled-back candidate proves the result.
