\section{Exact mathematical statement}
The declared reference-minus-candidate field vanishes exactly when the two operator fields are
equal:
\[
  \operatorname{Diff}(H_{\mathrm{ref}},H_{\mathrm{cand}})=0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  H_{\mathrm{ref}}=H_{\mathrm{cand}}.
\]

\section{Assumptions and conventions}
Both fields act on the same complex fiber. Equality and zero are exact function and
continuous-linear-map equalities, not tolerance-level numerical predicates.

\section{Mathematical argument}
For the forward direction, evaluate the zero-field equality at each point. The resulting
continuous-linear-map equality says the pointwise difference is zero, so
$H_{\mathrm{ref}}(k)=H_{\mathrm{cand}}(k)$. Function extensionality gives field equality. For
the reverse direction, substitute equal fields; every pointwise self-difference is zero.
