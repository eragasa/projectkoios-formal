\section{Exact statement and proof}
If $\operatorname{tr}D_i=0$ and $U_i$ is unitary, then
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(U_i^\dagger D_iU_i)=0.
\]
Cyclicity of the finite matrix trace moves $U_i$ next to $U_i^\dagger$; unitarity reduces
their product to the identity.
