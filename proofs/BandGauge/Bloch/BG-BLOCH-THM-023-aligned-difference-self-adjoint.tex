\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Under complete complex reference and candidate fibers, let $R_k$ and $C_k$ be pointwise self-adjoint and let $U_k$ be a supplied unitary alignment.  The aligned difference
\[
  \Delta_k=R_k-U_k^{-1}C_kU_k
\]
is self-adjoint for every $k$.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
By \texttt{BG-BLOCH-THM-022}, $U_k^{-1}C_kU_k$ is self-adjoint.  By \texttt{BG-BLOCH-THM-021}, subtracting it from the self-adjoint $R_k$ leaves a self-adjoint operator.  These pointwise conclusions give the field statement.
