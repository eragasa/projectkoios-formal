\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a supplied complex-linear isometric equivalence $U_k:E_{\mathrm{ref}}\to E_{\mathrm{cand}}$ and bounded candidate operator $C_k$, unitary pullback preserves the exact operator norm:
\[
 \lVert U_k^{-1}C_kU_k\rVert=\lVert C_k\rVert.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
For the forward inequality, apply the definition of operator norm and the action identity
\[
 \lVert U_k^{-1}C_kU_kv\rVert=\lVert C_kU_kv\rVert
 \leq\lVert C_k\rVert\lVert v\rVert.
\]
For the reverse inequality, write every candidate vector as $w=U_k(U_k^{-1}w)$ and apply the same argument to the pulled-back operator.  The inverse isometry preserves $\lVert w\rVert$, yielding both inequalities and hence equality.
