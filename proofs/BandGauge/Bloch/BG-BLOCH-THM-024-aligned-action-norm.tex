\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $U_k:E_{\mathrm{ref}}\to E_{\mathrm{cand}}$ be a supplied complex-linear isometric equivalence and let $C_k$ be a bounded complex-linear operator on the candidate fiber.  For every reference vector $v$,
\[
 \lVert U_k^{-1}C_kU_kv\rVert=\lVert C_kU_kv\rVert.
\]
