\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a positive even centered mesh, let $\mathcal M$ be its finite type of centered modes,
and let $\nu_-\in\mathcal M$ be the distinguished negative-Nyquist mode.  Define
\[
 S_-:=\mathcal M\setminus\{\nu_-\}
      =\operatorname{univ}.\operatorname{erase}(\nu_-).
\]
Then, with complement taken inside the complete finite type $\mathcal M$,
\[
 S_-^{\mathsf c}=\{\nu_-\}.
\]
The Lean declaration is \texttt{negativeNyquistRemovedSupport\_compl}; its source signature uses
Mathlib's finite-set complement notation.  The mathematical display above is the exact statement,
and the repository Lean source remains authoritative.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
For an arbitrary mode $m\in\mathcal M$,
\[
 m\in S_-^{\mathsf c}
 \iff m\notin\operatorname{univ}.\operatorname{erase}(\nu_-)
 \iff m=\nu_-
 \iff m\in\{\nu_-\}.
\]
The middle equivalence uses that every $m$ belongs to \texttt{univ}.  Extensionality over $m$
therefore proves equality of the two finite sets.  No arithmetic property of the integer
representative is needed after the distinguished mode has been constructed.
