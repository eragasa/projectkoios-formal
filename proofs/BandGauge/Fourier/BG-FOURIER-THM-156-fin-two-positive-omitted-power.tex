\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $G=(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2$, let $P_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$, and let
$H(k)=P_0$.  On the first momentum coordinate, define $U(k)$ to be the identity when
$k_0=0$ and the coordinate-swap matrix when $k_0\ne0$.  Put
$H_U(k)=U(k)^\dagger H(k)U(k)$.  For the repository's normalized finite Fourier transform,
\[
 \sum_{\chi\notin\{0\}}\sum_{i,j=0}^{1}
   \left\lVert\widehat{H_U}(\chi)_{ij}\right\rVert^2>0.
\]
This is the exact omitted matrix-Frobenius power after retaining only the trivial character.

\section{Proof structure}
The constant field is fixed by zero-mode truncation.  At all-zero momentum the varying gauge is the
identity, whereas at the momentum supported on the selected coordinate it is the swap; the gauged
field therefore takes the two distinct coordinate-projector values.  Hence zero-mode truncation
cannot commute with pointwise gauge conjugation, and the resulting truncation residual is nonzero.
A nonzero finite matrix field has strictly positive entrywise Frobenius power.  Exact finite matrix
Parseval identifies that residual power with $|G|$ times omitted coefficient power.  Since
$|G|>0$, the omitted power is strictly positive.
