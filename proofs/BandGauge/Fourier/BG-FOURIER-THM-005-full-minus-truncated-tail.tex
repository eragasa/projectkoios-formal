\section{Statement}
For coefficients in a complex module with additive inverses, full reconstruction minus support-truncated reconstruction is exactly the phase-weighted sum over omitted modes.
\section{Argument}
Apply the retained--omitted decomposition and cancel the retained term in the additive commutative group.
\section{Boundary and provenance}
This is an algebraic finite-tail identity, not Parseval, a norm equality, an error bound, or a continuum result. Lean checked the exact statement; the dossier and proof were AI-assisted.
