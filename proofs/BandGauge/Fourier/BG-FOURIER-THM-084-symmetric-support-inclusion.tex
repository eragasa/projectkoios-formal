\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let a positive even mesh have point count $N$ and half-count $h=N/2$.  Its centered integer
inventory is $[-h,h)\cap\mathbb Z$, its ordinary inventory deletes $-h$, and the symmetric
range-$R$ support is $[-R,R]\cap\mathbb Z$.  For every natural $R$,
\[
 R<h\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 [-R,R]\cap\mathbb Z
 \subseteq \bigl([-h,h)\cap\mathbb Z\bigr)\setminus\{-h\}.
\]
An ASCII rendering of the authoritative Lean signature (with finite-set order read as subset) is
\begin{verbatim}
theorem symmetricIntegerSupport_subset_ordinaryRepresentatives
    (mesh : CenteredEvenMesh) (maximumRange : Nat)
    (hRange : maximumRange < mesh.halfCount) :
    symmetricIntegerSupport maximumRange <= mesh.ordinaryRepresentatives
\end{verbatim}

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Take $m\in[-R,R]\cap\mathbb Z$.  Then $-R\le m\le R$.  Since $R<h$,
\[
 -h<-R\le m\le R<h.
\]
Consequently $m\in[-h,h)\cap\mathbb Z$ and $m\ne-h$.  These are exactly the two membership
conditions for $m$ to lie in the centered inventory with $-h$ erased.  Since $m$ was arbitrary,
the desired finite-set inclusion follows.
