\section{Exact statement and proof}
Let $\rho$ be a nonnegative radius on the finite character group such that
\[
 \rho(-p)=\rho(p),\qquad \rho(p+q)\leq \rho(p)+\rho(q).
\]
If the Fourier coefficients of $U$ vanish outside the complete shell of radius $R_U$ and those of
$A$ vanish outside the complete shell of radius $R_A$, then
\[
 \operatorname{supp}\widehat{U^\dagger A U}
 \subseteq \operatorname{shell}(R_A+2R_U).
\]
The existing triple-convolution support theorem places the coefficients in
$(-\operatorname{shell}(R_U)+\operatorname{shell}(R_A))+\operatorname{shell}(R_U)$.
Inversion invariance identifies the negated gauge shell with the original shell. Applying
subadditivity at the two support additions gives the cutoff
$(R_U+R_A)+R_U=R_A+2R_U$. Support monotonicity transfers the earlier coefficient-support theorem
to this larger complete shell.
