\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a positive even centered mesh with mode type $\mathcal M$ and distinguished mode
$\nu_-$.  Let $I$ be a nonempty finite index type and let
$C:\mathcal M\to\operatorname{Matrix}(I,I;\mathbb C)$.  Define
\[
 P_-(C):=\frac{1}{|I|}\sum_{i,j\in I}|C_{\nu_-}(i,j)|^2.
\]
If $S_-=\mathcal M\setminus\{\nu_-\}$ and
$w(m)=\sum_{i,j}|C_m(i,j)|^2$, then
\[
 P_-(C)=\frac{\operatorname{omittedWeight}(w,S_-)}{|I|}.
\]
This is the exact equality in \texttt{BG-FOURIER-THM-087}; it is not an inequality or an
approximation.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
By \texttt{BG-FOURIER-THM-081}, the complement of $S_-$ is $\{\nu_-\}$.  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{omittedWeight}(w,S_-)
   =\sum_{m\in S_-^{\mathsf c}}w(m)
   =\sum_{m\in\{\nu_-\}}w(m)
   =w(\nu_-).
\]
Expanding $w(\nu_-)$ gives the double entrywise norm-square sum in $P_-(C)$.  Dividing both
identical real expressions by $|I|$ yields the conclusion.
