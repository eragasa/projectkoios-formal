\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For finite square complex matrices $A,B$ and one unitary matrix $U$, define $\Phi_U(A)=U^{\dagger}AU$.  With the entrywise complex Frobenius inner product
\[
  \langle A,B\rangle_F=\sum_{i,j}\overline{A_{ij}}B_{ij},
\]
one has $\langle\Phi_U(A),\Phi_U(B)\rangle_F=\langle A,B\rangle_F$.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
First rewrite the entrywise inner product as $\operatorname{tr}(A^{\dagger}B)$.  The conjugated product reduces to
\[
 (U^{\dagger}AU)^{\dagger}(U^{\dagger}BU)=U^{\dagger}A^{\dagger}BU
\]
using $UU^{\dagger}=I$.  Cyclicity and unitary trace invariance then return $\operatorname{tr}(A^{\dagger}B)$.
