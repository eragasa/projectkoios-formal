\section{Exact mathematical statement}
With the same finite character inventory, support $S$, and fixed unitary $U$, the full-minus-truncated residual obeys
\[
 \rho_S(\Phi_U\circ c;g)=\Phi_U\bigl(\rho_S(c;g)\bigr).
\]
Here $\rho_S$ is exactly full reconstruction minus support-truncated reconstruction.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Expand the residual into full reconstruction minus truncated reconstruction.  Apply fixed-unitary equivariance to each reconstruction, then use linearity $\Phi_U(X-Y)=\Phi_U(X)-\Phi_U(Y)$.
