\section{Exact statement and proof}
If matrix coefficient fields $L$ and $R$ vanish outside finite supports $S$ and $T$, respectively,
then $L\star R$ vanishes outside the finite pointwise sum
\[
  S+T=\{s+t:s\in S,\ t\in T\}.
\]
For a mode outside $S+T$, each convolution summand vanishes: either its left index is outside $S$,
its right index is outside $T$, or the index sum is not the selected mode. Both finite sums are
therefore zero.
