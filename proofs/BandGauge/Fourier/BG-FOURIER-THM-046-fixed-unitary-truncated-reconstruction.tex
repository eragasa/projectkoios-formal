\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For coefficients $c_\chi$, a finite support $S$, momentum $g$, and one fixed unitary $U$, support truncation followed by finite reconstruction satisfies
\[
 \mathcal R_g\!\left(T_S(\Phi_U\circ c)\right)
 =\Phi_U\!\left(\mathcal R_g(T_Sc)\right),
 \qquad \Phi_U(A)=U^{\dagger}AU.
\]

\section{Assumptions, conventions, and statement delta}
The character inventory is finite, $S$ is the same exact support on both sides, and one $U$ is used for every coefficient and momentum.  Truncation is exact zeroing outside $S$.
The Lean proposition is authoritative.  This prose restatement neither strengthens the hypotheses
nor supplies an application binding.

\section{Statement-specific prior art and contribution boundary}
Commutation of a fixed linear map with a finite supported sum is standard \texttt{background-only} algebra.  The useful boundary is that the support is unchanged and the unitary is global.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
First commute fixed conjugation with exact support truncation coefficient by coefficient.  Then commute the same complex-linear map with the finite reconstruction sum.  Combining the two equalities yields the result.
