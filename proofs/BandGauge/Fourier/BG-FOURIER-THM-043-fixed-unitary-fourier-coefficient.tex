\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a finite additive group, $A:G\to M_n(\mathbb C)$, $\chi$ a finite character, and $U$ one fixed unitary.  Then normalized finite Fourier extraction commutes with conjugation:
\[
 \widehat{\bigl(U^{\dagger}AU\bigr)}(\chi)=U^{\dagger}\widehat A(\chi)U.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Conjugation by fixed $U$ is a complex-linear map $\Phi_U$.  Complex linearity moves $\Phi_U$ through the normalized finite sum defining the coefficient:
\[
 \Phi_U\!\left(\frac1{|G|}\sum_g \overline{\chi(g)}A_g\right)
 =\frac1{|G|}\sum_g\overline{\chi(g)}\Phi_U(A_g).
\]
Lean applies the generic linear-map/Fourier-coefficient theorem.
