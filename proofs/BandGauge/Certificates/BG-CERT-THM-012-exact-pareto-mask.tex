\section{Statement}
For a total exact finite objective table, the Boolean nondominated mask marks a decision
if and only if no declared decision dominates it.
\section{Argument}
The mask filters the complete finite decision enumeration by the proved exact dominance
Boolean and tests that the filter is empty. BG-CERT-THM-010 replaces Boolean acceptance
with propositional dominance. Empty-filter simplification then gives the definition of
Pareto efficiency.
