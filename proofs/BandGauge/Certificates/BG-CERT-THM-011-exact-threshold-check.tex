\section{Statement}
The Boolean threshold checker accepts exactly when one decision's value is no larger
than its budget in every objective.
\section{Argument}
The checker filters the complete finite objective enumeration by the budget inequality
and tests equality with the full enumeration. Finite-filter simplification converts this
test to the universal coordinatewise feasibility proposition.
