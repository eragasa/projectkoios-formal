\section{Exact statement}
Let $i:\Phi\to\mathcal H$ be a continuous injective map from the declared test space into a
complex Hilbert space with dense range. The assignment
\[\psi\longmapsto B_\psi,\qquad B_\psi(\phi)=\langle\psi,i\phi\rangle,
\]
from $\mathcal H$ to continuous linear functionals on $\Phi$ is injective.
\section{Source and assumptions}
This is source-neutral functional analysis. The test space is Hausdorff and locally convex and the
inclusion has dense range. Nuclearity, a concrete Schwartz space, and an operator spectral theorem
are not assumptions or conclusions.
\section{Proof schematic and proof}
If $B_\psi=B_\chi$, then the continuous functions
$y\mapsto\langle\psi,y\rangle$ and $y\mapsto\langle\chi,y\rangle$ agree on the dense image
$i(\Phi)$. Continuity extends equality to every $y\in\mathcal H$. Nondegeneracy of the inner
product in its right argument then gives $\psi=\chi$.
\section{Lean realization and trusted base}
The proof uses \texttt{DenseRange.equalizer} followed by \texttt{ext\_inner\_right}. The exact
functional orientation is fixed by Mathlib's conjugate-linear-first convention. Kernel-reported
axioms are \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}; there are no
admissions.
\section{Boundary and review}
Injectivity does not prove surjectivity onto the test dual, generalized eigenvector existence,
spectral completeness, or physical applicability. AI-assisted and kernel-checked; no independent
expert review is recorded.
