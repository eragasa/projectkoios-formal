\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For an invertible finite complex matrix $A$, vectors $u,v$, and $\gamma\in\mathbb C$,
\[
 \det\!\left(A+\gamma |u\rangle\langle v|\right)
 =\det(A)\left(1+\gamma\langle v|A^{-1}|u\rangle\right).
\]
Invertibility is encoded by \texttt{IsUnit A.det}.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Express $\gamma|u\rangle\langle v|$ as a replicated-column times replicated-row product.  Apply Mathlib's determinant formula for adding that product to an invertible matrix.  The resulting one-dimensional determinant is
$1+\gamma\langle v|A^{-1}|u\rangle$.
