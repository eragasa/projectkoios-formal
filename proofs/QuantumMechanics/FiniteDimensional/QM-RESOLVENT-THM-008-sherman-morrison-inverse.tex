\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $d=1+\gamma\langle v|A^{-1}|u\rangle$.  If $A$ is invertible and $d\ne0$, then
\[
 (A+\gamma|u\rangle\langle v|)^{-1}
 =A^{-1}-\frac{\gamma}{d}
   |A^{-1}u\rangle\langle v|A^{-1}.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
The preceding theorem constructs the displayed candidate and proves that multiplying it on the right by the updated matrix gives the identity.  The nonzero denominator and base invertibility imply the update determinant is a unit.  Uniqueness of left and right inverses then identifies the nonsingular inverse with the candidate.
