\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For invertible finite $A$, vector $v$, and complex magnitude $g$,
\[
 \det(A-g|v\rangle\langle v|)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 1-g\langle v|A^{-1}|v\rangle=0.
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Specialize the determinant factorization to coupling $-g$ and equal ket and bra.  Because $\det A\ne0$, the product
$\det A\,(1-g\langle v|A^{-1}|v\rangle)$ vanishes exactly when the scalar factor vanishes.
