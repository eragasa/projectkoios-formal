\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
\paragraph{Proof status.} Complete for the exact Lean proposition.
\paragraph{Argument.} Expand both definitions.  Associativity gives $C_J(A)C_J(B)=J^{\mathsf T}AJJ^{\mathsf T}BJ$.  Distribute multiplication over $I-JJ^{\mathsf T}$ on the right.  The identity term is $J^{\mathsf T}ABJ$ and the second term is the compressed-product term, so their difference is the claimed leakage expression.
