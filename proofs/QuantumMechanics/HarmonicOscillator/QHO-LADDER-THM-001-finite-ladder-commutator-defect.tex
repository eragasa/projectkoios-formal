\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
\paragraph{Proof status.} Complete for the exact Lean proposition.
\paragraph{Argument.} Compute both products entrywise.  The product $a_K^{\mathsf T}a_K$ is the diagonal number matrix.  The product $a_Ka_K^{\mathsf T}$ has diagonal entries $1,\ldots,K$ and zero at the last coordinate.  Subtraction gives $1$ below the boundary and $-K$ at the boundary, exactly the displayed identity-minus-projector matrix.
