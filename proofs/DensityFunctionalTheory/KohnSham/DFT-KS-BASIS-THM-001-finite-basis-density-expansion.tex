\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $B$ and $I$ be finite index types, let $X$ be any position carrier, and supply complex values
$\chi_\mu(r)$ and coefficients $C_{\mu i}$.  Define
\[
 \psi_i(r)=\sum_{\mu\in B} C_{\mu i}\chi_\mu(r)
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \rho(r)=\sum_{i\in I}|\psi_i(r)|^2.
\]
Then, for every $r\in X$,
\[
 \rho(r)=\sum_{i\in I}
   \left|\sum_{\mu\in B}C_{\mu i}\chi_\mu(r)\right|^2.
\]
Lean represents the squared complex modulus by \texttt{Complex.normSq}.

\section{Proof schematic}
\[
\begin{array}{c}
 \psi_i(r)=\sum_\mu C_{\mu i}\chi_\mu(r)\\[2mm]
 \rho(r)=\sum_i \operatorname{normSq}(\psi_i(r))\\
 \Downarrow\ \text{unfold the semantic bridge}\\
 \rho(r)=\sum_i\operatorname{normSq}\!\left(\sum_\mu C_{\mu i}\chi_\mu(r)\right).
\end{array}
\]

\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
The source-neutral module defines each reconstructed amplitude to be its finite coefficient-weighted
basis sum.  The Kohn--Sham bridge uses those amplitudes as an orbital family, and the established
unit-occupation density definition sums their squared complex moduli.  Substituting the first
definition into the second yields the displayed formula exactly.  No analytic or variational
argument is required.
