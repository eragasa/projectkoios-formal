\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let \(D\) be any density carrier, and let supplied real functionals be
\(F,T_s,E_H:D\to\mathbb R\).  Define
\[
 E_{xc}(\rho)=F(\rho)-T_s(\rho)-E_H(\rho)
\]
and reconstruct
\[
 F_{\rm KS}(\rho)=T_s(\rho)+E_H(\rho)+E_{xc}(\rho).
\]
Then, for every \(\rho\in D\),
\[
 F_{\rm KS}(\rho)=F(\rho).
\]

\section{Proof schematic}
\[
\begin{array}{c}
 E_{xc}=F-T_s-E_H\\
 \Downarrow\\
 F_{\rm KS}=T_s+E_H+(F-T_s-E_H)\\
 \Downarrow\ \text{exact real ring normalization}\\
 F_{\rm KS}=F
\end{array}
\]

\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
Expand the reconstructed internal energy and then unfold the exchange--correlation remainder.  The
positive and negative copies of \(T_s(\rho)\) cancel, as do the copies of \(E_H(\rho)\).  The only
remaining term is the supplied universal internal energy \(F(\rho)\).  No variational or analytic
argument enters this identity.
