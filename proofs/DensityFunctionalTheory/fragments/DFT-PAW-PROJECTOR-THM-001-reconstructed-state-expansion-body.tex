\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $V$ be a finite complex coordinate space and let $I$ be a finite channel set.  Supply
all-electron partial waves $\phi_i\in V$, pseudo partial waves $\widetilde\phi_i\in V$, and projector
vectors $\widetilde p_i\in V$.  Define
\[
 \Delta\phi_i=\phi_i-\widetilde\phi_i,
 \qquad
 T=I_V+\sum_{i\in I}|\Delta\phi_i\rangle\langle\widetilde p_i|.
\]
Then every pseudo state $\widetilde\psi\in V$ satisfies
\[
 T\widetilde\psi
 =\widetilde\psi+
   \sum_{i\in I}\langle\widetilde p_i|\widetilde\psi\rangle\Delta\phi_i.
\]
Projector duality and partial-wave completeness are not premises of this action identity.

\section{Proof schematic}
\[
\begin{aligned}
 T\widetilde\psi
 &=\left(I_V+\sum_i|\Delta\phi_i\rangle\langle\widetilde p_i|\right)
   \widetilde\psi\\
 &=\widetilde\psi+
   \sum_i |\Delta\phi_i\rangle
   \langle\widetilde p_i|\widetilde\psi\rangle\\
 &=\widetilde\psi+
   \sum_i\langle\widetilde p_i|\widetilde\psi\rangle\Delta\phi_i.
\end{aligned}
\]

\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
Distribute matrix-vector action over the identity plus the finite correction sum.  The identity
matrix returns the pseudo state.  Each rank-one matrix
$|\Delta\phi_i\rangle\langle\widetilde p_i|$ sends the state to the scalar projector amplitude
$\langle\widetilde p_i|\widetilde\psi\rangle$ times $\Delta\phi_i$.  Summing these channel
contributions gives the result.
