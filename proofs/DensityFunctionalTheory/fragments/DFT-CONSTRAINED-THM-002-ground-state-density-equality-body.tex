\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let a variational model have density map \(n\), internal energy \(F\), and external energy
\(E_{\rm ext}\).  For a representable density \(\rho\), let
\[
 \mathcal F(\rho)=\inf\{F(\phi):n(\phi)=\rho\},\qquad
 \mathcal E_v(\rho)=\mathcal F(\rho)+E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho).
\]
If \(\psi\) is a ground state for \(v\), then its represented density satisfies
\[
 \mathcal E_v(n(\psi))
 =F(\psi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,n(\psi)).
\]
The representable-density object on the left carries \(\psi\) as its witness.

\section{Proof schematic}
\[
\begin{array}{c}
 \psi\text{ is a ground state}\\
 \Downarrow\ \text{compare only states }\phi\text{ with }n(\phi)=n(\psi)\\
 F(\psi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,n(\psi))
 \le F(\phi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,n(\psi))\\
 \Downarrow\\
 F(\psi)\le F(\phi)\\
 \Downarrow\\
 \mathcal F(n(\psi))=F(\psi)\\
 \Downarrow\\
 \mathcal E_v(n(\psi))=E_v(\psi)
\end{array}
\]

\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
Take any state \(\phi\) having the same density as the supplied ground state \(\psi\).  Ground-state
minimality compares their total energies.  Since both external-energy terms are evaluated at the
same density, they cancel, giving \(F(\psi)\le F(\phi)\).  Thus \(\psi\) is a constrained-search
minimizer in its own density fiber.  Its internal energy is both a member and a lower bound of that
nonempty fiber, so it equals the real infimum.  Adding the common external-energy term gives the
stated total-energy equality.
