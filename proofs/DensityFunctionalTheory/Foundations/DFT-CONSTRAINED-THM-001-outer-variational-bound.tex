\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let a variational model supply a state-to-density map \(n\), internal energy \(F\), and external
energy \(E_{\rm ext}\).  For a density \(\rho\) represented by at least one state, define
\[
 \mathcal F(\rho)=\inf\{F(\phi):n(\phi)=\rho\}
\]
using the real \texttt{sInf}, and define
\(\mathcal E_v(\rho)=\mathcal F(\rho)+E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\).
If \(\psi\) is a ground state for \(v\), then every representable density satisfies
\[
 F(\psi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,n(\psi))\le \mathcal E_v(\rho).
\]
The ground-state inequality itself supplies a real lower bound for the fixed-density fiber, so the
conditionally complete real infimum is used only in a bounded-below setting in this theorem.

\section{Proof schematic}
\[
\begin{array}{c}
 \psi\text{ ground state},\quad n(\phi)=\rho\\
 \Downarrow\\
 E_v(\psi)\le F(\phi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\\
 \Downarrow\\
 E_v(\psi)-E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\le F(\phi)
 \quad\text{for every }F(\phi)\text{ in the fiber}\\
 \Downarrow\ \texttt{le\_csInf}\\
 E_v(\psi)-E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\le\mathcal F(\rho)\\
 \Downarrow\\
 E_v(\psi)\le\mathcal E_v(\rho)
\end{array}
\]

\section{Human-readable mathematical argument}
Fix a representable trial density \(\rho\).  Every energy in its internal-energy fiber comes from a
state \(\phi\) with \(n(\phi)=\rho\).  Ground-state minimality gives
\(E_v(\psi)\le F(\phi)+E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\), hence
\(E_v(\psi)-E_{\rm ext}(v,\rho)\le F(\phi)\).  This common lower bound applies to every member of
the nonempty fiber, so it is below the fiber infimum.  Adding back the external term gives the
claimed outer variational inequality.  The same lower bound also proves the fiber is bounded below.
