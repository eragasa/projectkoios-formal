\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For a finite ordered loop of vectors and independent unit-modulus vertex phases, the principal argument of the trace of the ordered rank-one state-matrix product is unchanged:
\[
 \arg\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\prod_x |p_x\psi_x\rangle\langle p_x\psi_x|\right)
 =\arg\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\prod_x |\psi_x\rangle\langle\psi_x|\right).
\]
