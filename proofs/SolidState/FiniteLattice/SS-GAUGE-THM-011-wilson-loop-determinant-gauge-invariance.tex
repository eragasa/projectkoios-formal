\section{Exact mathematical statement}
For finite square complex link matrices $L_{xy}$ and pointwise unitary frames $G_x$, form the ordered closed Wilson product.  Under
$L_{xy}\mapsto G_x^{-1}L_{xy}G_y$, its determinant is invariant:
\[
 \det W(L^G)=\det W(L).
\]

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Telescoping of internal frame factors gives $W(L^G)=G_b^{-1}W(L)G_b$ at the base vertex $b$.  Multiplicativity of determinant yields
$\det(G_b^{-1})\det W\det(G_b)$, and the first and last factors multiply to one.
