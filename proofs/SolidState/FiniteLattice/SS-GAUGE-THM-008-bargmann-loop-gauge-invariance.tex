\section{Exact mathematical statement}
Let $\psi_x$ be vectors in a complex inner-product space and let $p_x\in U(1)$ be independent vertex phases.  For any finite ordered closed loop,
\[
 \prod_{(x,y)\ \mathrm{along\ loop}}
   \langle p_x\psi_x,p_y\psi_y\rangle
 =\prod_{(x,y)\ \mathrm{along\ loop}}\langle\psi_x,\psi_y\rangle.
\]

The loop is represented by a base vertex and finite tail list.  Phases have unit modulus.  States need not be normalized and overlaps need not be nonzero.  Mathlib's inner product is conjugate-linear in the first argument.

nor supplies an application binding.

\section{Proof structure and mathematical argument}
Each transformed link acquires $p_x^{-1}$ on the left and $p_y$ on the right.  In the ordered product, internal factors cancel telescopically.  Closure cancels the final phase with the base phase, leaving the original product.
