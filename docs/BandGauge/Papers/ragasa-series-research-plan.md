# Ragasa series research plan

This plan coordinates Papers A--D without transferring proof, evidence, or publication status. Papers A--C have audited owner paths; Paper D does not.

- Author: Eugene Joseph M. Ragasa
- License: Apache-2.0
- Initial document completed: 2026-10-19

This is a research plan, not a theorem inventory, publication source, numerical result, or execution/publication authorization. Canonical bibliography identity remains owned by `projectkoios-references`.

## Shared thesis

Band agreement is not enough to establish an operator-faithful local reduced model. The series separates four questions.

| Paper | Question | Primary object |
|---|---|---|
| A | Are spectral and operator fidelity compatible? | admissible-set geometry |
| B | Why does reduction generate nonlocality, and how does dimension affect it? | Fourier and support geometry |
| C | How much gauge expressivity is required to recover locality? | finite-rank alignment geometry |
| D | Can the framework select a practical model from first-principles data? | certified oracle-to-local reduction |

## Shared rules

All papers follow the same ownership rules.

- Reusable mathematics belongs in semantic modules.
- Paper namespaces remain thin source contracts and instantiations.
- Every paper freezes its rank, dimension, scalar field, mesh, weights, Fourier convention, support hierarchy, alignment family, and thresholds.
- One common decision must satisfy all declared channels.
- Formal proof, certificate status, runtime evidence, applicability, novelty, and publication status remain independent.
- No paper inherits evidence from another without an explicit bridge.

## Paper A — compatibility geometry

Purpose: show that spectral acceptance can coexist with operator rejection.

Formal state:

- conditional source-specific loss bridges are proved;
- exact rank-two separation checkers are proved;
- one exact two-observation analytic construction is proved;
- no calculated four-state-parent result is bound.

Next gate: freeze and independently verify an owner-controlled exact or outward-rounded runtime certificate.

Detailed plan: `Ragasa2026A/paper-and-proof-plan.md`.

## Paper B — projection-induced nonlocality

Purpose: explain why projection creates long-range coefficients and quantify support cost across dimension and symmetry.

Required outputs:

- source-faithful projection/nonlocality theorem;
- coefficient and tail formulas;
- complete-shell finite decision table;
- exact or interval objectives;
- independently checked Pareto and minimum-cost conclusions.

Current state: generic Fourier and finite-decision foundations exist; paper-specific projection, special-function, table, and evidence layers do not.

Detailed plan: `Ragasa2026B/paper-and-proof-plan.md`.

## Paper C — gauge expressivity

Purpose: compare identity, global, structured local, transport/polar, and exact-inverse alignments across retained rank, depth, and dimension.

Required outputs:

- normalized D1/D2/D3 case matrix;
- exact P0/G0 controls;
- singular-gap and endpoint-sewing diagnostics;
- Fourier reconstruction and truncation checks;
- first common support satisfying spectral and operator thresholds; and
- retained independently verified evidence.

Current state: paper namespace is metadata-only. The owner-side development calculator is schema-free and not production evidence.

Detailed plan: `Ragasa2026C/paper-and-proof-plan.md`.

## Paper D — first-principles application

Purpose: bind a retained first-principles oracle to one finite-support local model under both channels.

Entry requirements:

- owner-approved immutable protocol;
- selected physical system;
- frozen oracle, retained-subspace, alignment, support, and loss conventions;
- complete provenance and independent replay;
- exact certificate boundary; and
- separate authorization.

No Paper D namespace or source key exists.

Detailed plan: `Ragasa2026D/paper-and-proof-plan.md`.

## Program order

The series advances only after each upstream evidence gate.

1. Finish the Paper A owner-side certificate contract and one retained checked instance.
2. Formalize Paper B projection/support results and freeze an exhaustive objective-table schema.
3. Complete the Paper C fail-closed D1 calculator and independent verifier.
4. Extend Paper C to native D2/D3 cases only after source review.
5. Add continuum and special-function layers only when a selected statement requires them.
6. Define Paper D only after its owner protocol exists.

## Review gates

Each publication claim requires separate findings for:

- statement fidelity;
- theorem and axiom status;
- certificate coverage;
- runtime provenance;
- scientific interpretation;
- novelty and citations; and
- publication authorization.

The series is a plan, not a publication record.
